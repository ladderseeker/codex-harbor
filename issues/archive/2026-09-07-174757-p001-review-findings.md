| Finding | Reproduction or evidence | Impact / acceptance |

## Round 2 findings and progress

Round 1's five supervisor regressions passed in the expanded real-stack E2E run. The revised fixed HTTP gateway passed its network fronting regressions, and independent round 2 found no further gateway defect. These results do not close the entire issue.

Round 2 identified three further High defects: intentionally closing an idle runtime could mark its original completed turn uncertain; idempotency cleanup could remove a record while its admitted future-skewed timestamp remained retryable; and an untracked discovery runtime could restore an old credential after removal. Fixes require completed-turn/profile-change and restart checks, maintenance plus future-skew retry checks, and a delayed discovery/login versus credential-removal race test.

Additional review covered captured approval identity, premature credential IPC EOF, account status refresh, and replay-gap EventSource recreation. The frontend replay fix has been reviewed. Visual inspection found mobile navigation clipping and an obscured approval heading; fixes are awaiting fresh screenshot evidence. D003 review also requires unique filesystem/project-quota IDs and reserved-headroom admission, in addition to actual hard-limit enforcement.

## Round 3 findings and progress

Updated mobile screenshots show the navigation and conversation without clipping. The expanded fixture E2E run `harbor-e2e-33491d7752` passed credential replacement/removal, process/storage UI, expiry, offline approval, logout, and earlier lifecycle regressions. Round 3 found two remaining High security defects: a retiring or unsuccessfully closed runtime could outlive credential removal's sweep; and persisted browser sessions lacked issuer/subject binding, allowing an old-owner cookie to survive owner-pin reconfiguration. Track unconfirmed transports until confirmed cleanup, and bind every session and downstream authorization check to the original identity. Required regressions cover removal during asynchronous retirement and old-cookie rejection after an owner configuration change. These critical fixes remain active.

Medium completeness findings concern exact replay retention bounds and cancellation-operation quota admission. The implementer is assessing bounded corrections; any remaining obligations must retain explicit ownership before this issue closes. D003's quota-ID, headroom, and socket ancestry checks are now present by source review; actual XFS evidence is still pending.

## Round 4 critical fix verification

The focused review found the owner-binding and runtime-retirement fixes sound by source inspection. The real-stack E2E run `harbor-e2e-072b9eaf9e` passed the owner-configuration change and discovery-initialization cleanup regressions, including the final captured-transport cleanup correction. Its source fingerprints changed during independent isolation-test edits, so a stable final source identity is still being recorded.

Actual Linux testing exposed a pinned-runtime ordering detail: `turn/start` can return before `turn/started` establishes the active turn. The adapter now waits within a bounded deadline for the exact turn before interruption. Review then found that an already-completed turn, disconnect, or activation timeout could leave the cancellation operation in `dispatching`. The same fix must settle that operation even if its database transaction initially fails, while preserving uncertainty of the original turn. This critical correction and its focused regressions remain in progress.

The canonical Linux XFS lane has passed actual owner/API allocation, runner permissions, hard byte/inode quotas, inheritance denials, native interruption and process inspection, history resume across generations, root replacement denial, generation retirement, and the fixed gateway. The separate gateway lane also passed in the dedicated VM. Final source/artifact evidence and the last cancellation correction remain pending before this issue can close. Authenticated model acceptance remains in the separate live-account issue.
